Changes

- Missing authoritative period data is explicitly flagged by the validator.
- The batch report has a one-row-per-chart diagnosis table with bias, phase,
  current price, Fib anchors and levels, period H/L roles, entries and review flags.
- A review-only filter makes 10-20 chart batches easier to scan.
- Fibonacci validation uses the selector's one-percent boundary allowance and
  no longer fails an entry merely because its customer-facing price is rounded.
