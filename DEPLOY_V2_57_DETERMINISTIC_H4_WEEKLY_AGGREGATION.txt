CSA BENCHMARK v2.57 — DETERMINISTIC H4 WEEKLY AGGREGATION

Deploy the complete contents of this folder.

Authoritative structural inventory
- M1, M5, M15, M30 and H1: read every D1 candle high and low inside the
  current trading week, in chronological order, up to the visible cutoff.
- H4: read every W1 candle high and low inside the current calendar month,
  in chronological order (W1, W2, W3, W4 and W5 when present), up to the
  visible cutoff.
- Do not skip, merge or renumber periods.
- Complete this inventory before checking S/R, S/D or Fibonacci confluence.

Fibonacci remains a separate step
- M1-H1: current calendar-week high and low.
- H4: current calendar-month high and low.
- Fibonacci qualifies structure; it never replaces the individual D1/W1
  candle inventory and never creates a level.

Automatic benchmark enforcement
- A dated H1 chart must return every visible D1 period in its current week.
- A dated H4 chart must return every visible W1 period in its current month.
- Missing periods cause Needs Review before entry selection is accepted.
- The report displays the D1/W1 inventory independently of the Fibonacci audit.

v2.56 correction
- Focused D1/W1 inventory survives the merge even when that reader finds no entry.
- A complete inventory authoritatively reconstructs the current-period Fib frame:
  highest inventory high to lowest inventory low.
- An incomplete inventory still fails safely and cannot authorize an entry.

v2.57 correction
- H4 users upload one H4 chart only; no companion W1 chart is required.
- Visible H4 candles are grouped into Monday-Friday W1 periods automatically.
- An opening weekend is not counted as a separate week, so August 3-7 is W1.
- Final-visible price synchronization cannot rewind to a much older candle
  merely because that candle has a similar closing price.
