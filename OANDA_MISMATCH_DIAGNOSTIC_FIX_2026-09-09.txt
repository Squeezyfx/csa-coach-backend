OANDA mismatch diagnostic fix — 2026-09-09

An unfinished final close is now deferred only when provider O/H/L aligns within tolerance. If the provider high, low, or open also disagrees, the result is a hard provider/chart mismatch; the unfinished-close buffer cannot hide it. Diagnostics include mismatchFields, closeMismatch, and ohlMismatch.

This improves the explanation without forcing unverified Fib levels or entries. OANDA remains the primary provider.
