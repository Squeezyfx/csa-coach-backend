CSA Benchmark v2.53 — current-period enforcement and header coverage

This update fixes three header aliases:
- Cocoa,Daily → COCOA
- Platinum,Daily → XPTUSD
- USA500.S,Daily → US500

It also enforces the CSA Fibonacci period rule for new benchmark charts:
- H1 and lower: visible current calendar week
- H4: visible current calendar month
- D1/W1: visible current calendar year

The reader must be able to see the beginning of the required period. A D1
chart that starts in June, for example, is now labelled Needs review instead
of silently using a partial year or an older local swing. The benchmark card
now shows its exact selected Fib frame high and low beside the bias.
