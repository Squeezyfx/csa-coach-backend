What changed
- The focused chart reader receives the exact start date for every required D1, W1 or MN candle.
- Inventory verification rejects missing, shifted and extra periods.
- Each high/low must be the highest/lowest wick only inside that exact period.
- The verified fixed-period structure controls bias; the last sharp candle is treated as a pullback unless the controlling structure actually fails.
- Valid structure just outside a Fibonacci band edge may use only a tightly capped 1% of impulse-range rounding allowance.
- Independently verified chart prices remain authoritative while external-data disagreements stay visible in the audit.
- The duplicate lower-quality period-frame vision request was removed; one full-quality focused read supplies both inventory and Fibonacci frame.
