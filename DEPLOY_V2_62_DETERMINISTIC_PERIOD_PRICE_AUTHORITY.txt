Authority rule
- Verified D1/W1/MN candles control every period high and low.
- The exact chart-header final candle is reconciled into the final period.
- The period inventory then controls fixed-period bias and the Fibonacci high/low.
- Vision-estimated period prices are comparison evidence only.
- A vision inventory is not verified merely because it echoes the requested dates.
- When deterministic period candles are unavailable, the benchmark returns no verified frame and no entry instead of guessing.
- Material chart-versus-data differences remain displayed for diagnosis.
